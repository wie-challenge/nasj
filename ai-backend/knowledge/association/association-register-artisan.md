# Register an artisan

Use this page to add a rural woman artisan your association works with to the NASJ registry. She does not need an email address, a smartphone, a password or an account of her own.

## What you need

- Her name, region and language.
- Her equipment and capabilities, and her skills.
- Her availability.
- Her participation authorization: the approved method, the date and an evidence reference. A checkbox alone is not accepted. A documented verbal authorization also needs a witness reference.

## Steps

1. Open the new artisan page (`/association/artisans/new`).
2. Fill in her details and skills.
3. Record her participation authorization.
4. Save.

## Good to know

- A phone number is optional.
- If a similar artisan already exists, the new record is blocked until a NASJ operator reviews it.
- Declaring her weekly capacity is a separate step and needs its own capacity authorization.
- Only her assigned coordinator can see her private record.
