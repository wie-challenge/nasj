## Compact decision ledger

- **D13-01 — Select #209 only.** Receipt → private assessment → named follow-up → existing case/history is usable without selling an unapproved contract or inventing paid cohorts.
- **D13-02 — Add feedback, reuse claims.** SupportTicket cannot express satisfaction/receipt/versioned assessment fields; OrderDiscussion is free text. Add only a structured feedback aggregate and append-only revisions within collaboration.
- **D13-03 — Preserve authority boundaries.** Support access does not imply buyer authorship; internal organization membership does not alone grant feedback review. Formal case status and all financial remedies remain with their current authorities.
- **D13-04 — Do not release recurring orders through a provenance gap.** Existing reorder creates a fresh RFQ but no proposal link. The finance gate currently applies to linked proposals (including historical JSON) or explicit review. Future recurring RFQs must carry immutable applicability into ProcurementService.accept even if no salesperson creates a proposal; stage changes cannot disable it. This is a future integration requirement, not a reproduced regression in A12-02.
- **D13-05 — No paid-customer inference.** Any later #208 report needs event-time cohorts, explicit denominators, evidence classes, unknowns and suppression. Until authorized actual evidence exists, paid/retention/CAC results remain unavailable rather than zero or inferred from quotes.
- **D13-06 — Policy gates remain separate.** Actual revenue/cost recognition, invoice policy, verified settlement authority, lawful financial closure, retention/legal review and E19-01 participant comprehension remain open. All owner acceptances are PENDING. No production or live-pilot readiness is claimed.
