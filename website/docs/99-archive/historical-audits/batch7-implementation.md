## D. Financial simulation

`packages/contracts/src/scenario-finance.ts` is a pure calculation module. `FinancialSimulator` is a client component shared by `/simulator` and presentation scene 9. It neither calls commercial APIs nor stores its assumptions. The public model needs no permission grant because it contains only user-entered fictional assumptions and no private records. No migration is added.

Inputs are whole quantity (0–1,000,000 pieces), exact TND amounts (0–1,000,000 TND per entered amount), unit or hourly compensation mode, whole effort minutes per piece (0–100,000), materials/QA/packaging per piece, fixed transport/overhead per order, and rejection/contingency percentages (0–100 with two decimals). Missing, malformed or out-of-range values withhold that scenario's results and identify the invalid field. Baseline, optimistic, conservative and custom are explicitly synthetic.

- Unit mode uses the entered artisan compensation per piece. Hourly mode uses rate × effort minutes / 60, rounded HALF_UP once to a millime per piece. Estimated effective pay/hour = unit compensation × 60 / effort minutes; zero effort or quantity is undefined. Effort/rates are unmeasured assumptions, not approved policy or verified income.
- Expected revenue = quantity × selling price × (1 − rejection%). Rejection is a modeled unsold-piece revenue haircut. **All produced pieces retain their full compensation, material, QA and packaging costs**, including modeled rejected pieces; no wage deduction is inferred.
- Variable costs = quantity × (compensation + materials + QA + packaging). Fixed costs = transport + overhead. Contingency = all these costs × contingency%. Revenue and contingency each round HALF_UP once at the millime boundary. Contribution = revenue − modeled costs; losses remain signed TND. Percentage outputs round HALF_UP to 0.01%, with zero-revenue ratios undefined. No tax, financing rate or payment timing is guessed.
- Estimated break-even = ceiling(fixed costs × (1 + contingency%) / (price × (1 − rejection%) − variable unit cost × (1 + contingency%))). This is the continuous planning threshold before revenue/contingency millime rounding. Nonpositive unit margin is undefined; zero fixed costs and a positive margin yield zero. It is not an accepted order quantity.
- Modeled producer share follows agreed compensation / net quotation revenue with zero modeled refunds, reusing `agreedProducerShare` and its rounding for supported values. Three explicitly hypothetical fictional artisans satisfy its cohort minimum; no real cohort is implied. Ratios above its public 1000% ceiling are retained as model arithmetic, explicitly outside that publication policy. The denominator is modeled net quotation revenue, not cash paid or verified income.

Baseline: 100 pieces; price 30.000; compensation 12.000; materials 5.000; QA 1.000; packaging 1.000 TND/piece; transport 80.000 and overhead 120.000 TND/order; contingency/rejection each 5%; effort 90 minutes. Results: expected revenue **2850.000**, compensation **1200.000**, costs **2205.000**, contribution **645.000 TND / 22.63%**, estimated break-even **25 pieces**, effective pay **8.000 TND/hour**. Conservative assumptions yield **−353.000 TND** contribution. These are examples of the implemented model, not business findings. Chart bars use calculated values with a zero axis; printed amounts provide accessible exact results. Reset restores baseline only in component memory.
