# Evidence provenance policy

Display classifications are PROPOSED (hypothesis/planned outcome), SYNTHETIC (generated, simulated or software demonstration data), MEASURED (source measurement required) and VERIFIED (approved independent review required). They do not replace legal, payment, custody, consent, quality or other domain states.

Shared `packages/contracts/src/provenance.ts` carries claimId/type, evidenceStatus, privacy-safe sourceReference/sourceType, optional scenario/timestamps/reviewer/policy metadata, limitations and canonical displayLabel. Caller-provided labels are not authority. Publication validates the complete contract and source compatibility. This release has no approved measurement or verification publication policy: both MEASURED and VERIFIED are rejected, even if callers type reviewer/policy fields. Synthetic/proposed claims cannot carry fabricated verification timestamps or identities.

`EvidenceNotice` shows the canonical label, source and limitations on public and relevant application surfaces. The public /evidence page explains methodology and missing field sources, and /presentation retains those warnings. Existing directory, feasibility/costing, QR/producer-share, payment simulations and report exports receive shared notices/metadata. No absent impact dashboard is claimed implemented. CSV/JSON exports preserve classification, source and limitations; screenshot/presentation content displays material warnings in the same rendered surface. An arbitrary displayLabel passed to the export helper is recomputed, preventing a synthetic source from keeping a forged “verified” caption.

Private source evidence is not published to justify a label. Public references are allowlisted methodology anchors, not arbitrary private document URLs or personal IDs. QR content continues to reject unsafe story details and exposes coarse craft provenance only. Scenario IDs identify synthetic instances and never create access authority. Public claims of interviews, income benefit, medical effects, partner endorsements, active participant counts or physical/bank verification remain unavailable without approved sources.

Evidence register: GitHub acceptance snapshot and executed test logs are engineering source evidence. Software test outcomes do not establish field-measured commercial effects. Organizer-format verification was skipped at owner request and remains pending; technical report/video drafts explicitly say so. Future evidence-source policies must be approved, server-owned, versioned and scoped before new measured/verified publication transitions are added.
