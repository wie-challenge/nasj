# Role responsibilities and story access review

Reviewed 2026-10-22. This defines product access responsibility, not GitHub assignees or engineering ownership. Canonical story IDs, issue states and dependencies are unchanged.

| Role | Available workspace and responsibility | Access boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform admin | `/admin`: all account administration, organization approval requests, coordinator/operator invitations and affiliations, account assignments, authorization-policy review, privacy requests, independent participant concerns and audits | Admin status does not grant commercial execution, participant private operations or payouts. Commercial RFQs belong to assigned operators. |
| NASJ operator | `/operator`: coordinate assigned buyers and partner organizations; `/procurement`: review submitted RFQs, clarify terms, record exact sample references and prepare quotations | Only assigned approved accounts. Partner review exposes minimal participant references, not contacts or individual finances. |
| Buyer representative | `/buyer`: own company; `/procurement`: requests, references, revisions, samples, quotes, discussions, documents and reorders | Company scope; quotation/sample sign-off requires explicit approval authority. No user administration or association registry access. |
| Association coordinator | `/association`: add authorized artisans without creating logins, manage assigned records, record declared capacity and authorized history; permitted order discussions | One active association affiliation; only assigned artisans. No buyer prices, procurement documents or internal notes. |

An organization is a tenant, not a shared login. Each coordinator signs in with a personal account. Artisans can participate as managed records without credentials. Historical enum roles/accounts remain for existing references and explicitly scoped legacy simulations; they are not new core personas.

The local seed creates the four current login personas, assigns the operator to both the synthetic buyer and association, and explicitly grants the synthetic buyer quotation approval. Re-seeding preserves current passwords and does not restore intentionally revoked assignments or grants.
